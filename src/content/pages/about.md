  - question: Can you help plan my wedding décor and entertainment?
    answer: "Yes. We help you plan everything we supply, from the mandap and venue styling to the DJ, dance floor, lighting and effects. Send us your date, venue and ideas and we will build a bespoke package around them, working closely with your venue and planners so the day feels like one considered design. With a single point of contact, planning the styling and entertainment side of your wedding stays simple."
  - question: Where do you work?
    answer: "We are based in Leicester and cover Leicestershire and the wider East and West Midlands, including Birmingham, Nottingham, Derby and Coventry. We also work in London, Yorkshire and the North West, and can travel to venues elsewhere in the UK. Message us your venue and we will confirm."
  - question: What services do you offer?
    answer: "Mandap hire and stage styling, civil ceremony décor, reception décor and table centrepieces, Haldi and Mehndi décor for homes, marquees and banquet halls, wedding DJ and entertainment, LED dance floor and lighting hire, outdoor house and shopfront lighting, and cold sparklers and dry ice low fog. They can be booked individually or combined into one bespoke package."
  - question: What is included in a mandap hire package?
    answer: "A full ceremony mandap setup includes 4 choris, 2 bajots and patlas, 6 chairs, a havan kund, a Ganesh statue for the foyer, an entrance gate, a pleated backdrop for the mandap, and carpet for the mandap and walkway. We design traditional carved wooden and modern gold geometric mandaps, so tell us your style and venue."
  - question: Do you style Hindu, Sikh, Muslim and civil weddings?
    answer: "Yes. Our styling covers Asian, Hindu, Sikh, Muslim and civil celebrations as well as multicultural weddings, and our DJs mix Bhangra, Bollywood, R&B, pop and more to suit your guests."
  - question: Are cold sparklers and dry ice fog safe indoors?
    answer: "Our cold spark machines are non-pyrotechnic, with no flame, smoke or burning smell, and our dry ice low fog stays low to the floor for a dancing-on-clouds first dance. We liaise with your venue and complete a risk assessment, and trained technicians operate the effects on the day."
  - question: How do I get a quote?
    answer: "Message us on WhatsApp or send an enquiry through the contact form with your date, venue and ideas, and we will get back to you. Quotations stay valid for 14 days, and your date is not reserved until your booking is confirmed."
  - question: How do I book, and how much is the deposit?
    answer: "A booking is confirmed once you accept your quotation and pay the agreed deposit, which is set out in your quotation. Our terms set a minimum of 25% of the total booking price or £500. The remaining balance is due 14 days before your event."
  - question: How early do you need access to the venue?
    answer: "It depends on your package and set-up, and on your venue's policies. For mandap and stage set-ups we typically need access around 3 to 4 hours before your guests arrive, with clear loading, parking and lift facilities. If your venue allows it, we can also set up the evening or night before a morning event. Tell us your venue and we will plan the timings with you."
