## 1. Booking Confirmation & Payment Terms

1. **Quotation Validity**: Quotations provided by JNB Events remain valid for **14 calendar days** from the date of issue. Dates are not reserved until a formal booking is completed.
2. **Booking Confirmation**: A booking is formally confirmed once the Client accepts the quotation, signs or electronically approves the agreement, and pays the agreed initial deposit.
3. **Deposit Purpose**: The deposit (minimum **25%** of the total booking price or £500, as specified in your quotation) is non-refundable, subject to the Client's statutory consumer rights. It secures your exclusive event date in our calendar and allows JNB Events to begin planning, ordering bespoke items, and allocating equipment and crew.
4. **Final Balance**: The remaining balance must be paid in full no later than **14 calendar days prior to the event date**. For bookings made within 14 days of the event date, 100% of the total price is due immediately upon booking.
5. **Additional Services**: Any additional services, items, or changes requested after booking confirmation will be quoted separately and must be paid before installation.

## 2. Cancellation & Refund Policy

All cancellations must be submitted in writing via email or written communication.

Because event styling involves reserving bespoke inventory, custom floral planning, and turning away other clients for the same date, cancellation charges apply as follows:

- **More than 90 days before the event date**: The initial deposit is retained. Any additional balance payments made are refunded in full.
- **30 to 90 days before the event date**: The deposit is retained, plus **50% of the remaining balance** to cover committed stock, supplier reservations, and administrative costs.
- **Fewer than 30 days before the event date**: **100% of the total booking price** is payable and non-refundable, as all florals, materials, transport, and crew will have been committed.

*If JNB Events is able to rebook the identical date and service with another client, we will reasonably assess any partial refund or credit towards future services.*
