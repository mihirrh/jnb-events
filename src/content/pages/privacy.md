This is a plain-English summary of how this website handles your information.

## What this website collects

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies, and it does not track you across other websites.

If you send an enquiry, we receive the details you choose to share, such as your name, contact details, event date, venue and message. We use them only to reply to you and to plan your event.

## WhatsApp

Tapping a WhatsApp button opens WhatsApp on your device with a pre-written message. From that point your messages are handled by WhatsApp under its own privacy policy.

## Hosting

The website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like most hosts, Cloudflare may process technical information such as IP address and browser type to deliver and secure the site.

## Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Contact us on WhatsApp to make a request.
