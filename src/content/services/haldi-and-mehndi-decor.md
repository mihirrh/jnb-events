Pre-wedding ceremonies set the joyful tone for your entire celebration. At **JNB Events**, we design vibrant, immersive **Haldi and Mehndi décor** in Leicester and across the East and West Midlands that turns traditional rituals into breathtaking, photo-worthy occasions.

Whether you are hosting an intimate Haldi ceremony at home, a marquee garden party, or a high-energy Mehndi night in a luxury ballroom, our styling team brings creativity, colour harmony, and precision setup to every space.

## Haldi Ceremony Décor & Styling

The Haldi ceremony is full of warmth, laughter, and auspicious rituals. We create radiant setups centred around the bride and groom:

- **Marigold & Sunflower Backdrops**: Dense floral walls, draped curtains in shades of turmeric yellow, mustard, and lime, and hanging marigold tassels.
- **Traditional Urli Seating**: Polished brass and floral-adorned urli bowls with comfortable cushions, perfectly arranged for the turmeric paste ritual.
- **Decorative Props & Photo Points**: Embroidered Rajasthani parasols, painted earthen pots, and floral photo-frames that delight your guests.

## Mehndi Night Décor & Lounge Styling

Mehndi nights are dynamic celebrations filled with music, dancing, and intricate henna artistry. We provide comfortable, stylish lounges and statement stages:

- **Bespoke Floral Swings (Jhoolas)**: Beautifully decorated wooden or metal swings wrapped in lush foliage, roses, and marigolds as the signature focal point.
- **Low Floor Seating & Lounges**: Colourful mattress setups with bolsters, embroidered cushions, and low brass tables creating an inviting, relaxed atmosphere.
- **Vibrant Draping & Fairy Lighting**: Multicoloured ceiling drapes in fuchsia, teal, orange, and gold, enhanced with warm fairy lights and uplighting.
