Your evening reception should feel like a magnificent celebration from the moment guests enter the dining room. At **JNB Events**, we provide luxury **wedding reception décor in Leicester** and across the Midlands, creating cohesive table landscapes and stunning room transformations.

## Statement Table Centrepieces & Ambient Table Scapes

We offer a versatile collection of table centrepieces designed to suit high-ceiling ballrooms, marquee spaces, and intimate dining rooms:

- **Elevated Floral Stands**: Tall gold risers and acrylic plinths topped with overflowing floral globes of hydrangeas, roses, and trailing foliage, allowing effortless across-table conversation.
- **Crystal & Antique Gold Candelabras**: Classic multi-arm candelabras adorned with tapered candles and floral rings, adding height, drama, and warm flickering light.
- **Low Botanical & Candle Bowls**: Intimate low-profile floral arrangements surrounded by clusters of ribbed votives, floating cylinder candles, and textured greenery runners.
- **Sweetheart & Top Table Focus**: Breathtaking head table installations featuring cascading floral meadows, custom table linen, and coordinating backdrop lighting.
