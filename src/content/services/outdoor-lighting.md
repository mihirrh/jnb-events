In many Asian families the celebrations begin well before the ceremony, with the bride's or groom's house lit up for everyone to see. Along **Belgrave Road in Leicester**, shopfronts and homes are lit up in just the same way for festivals and celebrations. At **JNB Events**, we provide **outdoor wedding lighting and Asian wedding house lights in Leicester** and across the Midlands, designed to suit your property.

## House & Wedding Lighting

We dress the outside of the home so the whole street knows there is a celebration:
- **Fairy-Light Curtains**: Cascading strands across the front of the property, porch or garden wall.
- **Gates, Pillars & Driveways**: Lighting that welcomes your guests from the moment they arrive.
- **Gardens & Marquees**: Soft lighting for outdoor seating, pathways and marquee entrances.

## Shops & Business Frontages

The same lighting works for businesses that want to mark a festival or a special occasion. Tell us about your shopfront and the date, and we will plan a design to suit it.

## How It Works

1. **Tell us about the property**: Message us your address area, the date and a photo of the front of the house or shop.
2. **We plan the design**: We suggest a layout and confirm the details with you in your quotation.
3. **We install and take it down**: Our team handles the set-up before your event and the removal afterwards.
