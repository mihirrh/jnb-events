### What Can Be Included in Your Bespoke Package?

We tailor every bundle to your exact itinerary and venue layout:

1. **Ceremony Staging & Mandap Hire**:
   - Traditional carved wooden or contemporary gold geometric mandaps.
   - Handcrafted bridal seating, havan kund, and matching family chairs.
   - Gold mirrored or white carpet aisle runners, Ganesh pedestals, and entrance arches.

2. **Full Venue & Reception Décor**:
   - Statement table centrepieces (tall floral stands, candelabras, botanical bowls).
   - Sweetheart & top table backdrops, starlight draping, and welcome displays.
   - Coordinated charger plates, chair dressing, and ambient candlelight.

3. **DJ & Stage Entertainment Production**:
   - Award-winning Asian wedding DJ and bilingual master of ceremonies.
   - High-fidelity sound systems, wireless microphones, and intelligent moving-head light shows.
   - White gloss or twinkling LED starlit dance floors.

4. **Cinematic Special Effects**:
   - Indoor-safe cold sparkler fountains for grand entrances and cake cutting.
   - 'Dancing on Clouds' dry ice low fog for an enchanting first dance.

- **Cohesive Design**: Your ceremony styling, evening lighting, and reception décor share a unified colour palette and aesthetic standard.
- **Flawless Timing**: Our team manages the turnaround from daytime ceremony to evening party without awkward vendor clashes.
- **Budget Optimisation**: Enjoy substantial package savings over booking separate vendors individually.
