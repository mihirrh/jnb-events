Your civil wedding is the legal and emotional heart of your marriage. At **JNB Events**, we design refined **civil ceremony décor in Leicester** and across Leicestershire, transforming hotel ballrooms, country barns, registry suites, and estate gardens into romantic, photogenic settings.

## Romantic Ceremony Arches & Aisle Concepts

Whether your taste leans towards clean contemporary minimalism or lavish floral abundance, our civil ceremony setups include:

- **Bespoke Ceremony Arches & Backdrops**: Circular moongates, open-frame gold arches, and classic wooden arbours adorned with hydrangeas, roses, eucalyptus, and delicate draping.
- **Aisle Styling & Walkways**: Plush white, ivory, or mirrored carpet runners flanked by hurricane lanterns, floating cylinder candles, and low floral meadow boxes.
- **Registrar Table Presentation**: Coordinated low floral arrangements, bespoke tablecloths, and ceremonial pen styling designed for flawless signing photos.
- **Guest Seating & Chair Dressing**: Lime wash, gold, or mahogany Chiavari chairs with chiffon drapes, organza ties, or floral accents.

We coordinate seamlessly with Leicestershire registrars and venue management to ensure prompt setup and smooth turnaround before your evening reception begins. Contact our team via WhatsApp to discuss styling for your civil ceremony.
