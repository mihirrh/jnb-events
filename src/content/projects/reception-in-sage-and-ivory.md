Sage and ivory create a gentle foundation for a reception room, with floral centrepieces and candlelight adding colour and warmth to the tables.

Soft overhead lighting carries the palette across the room, while the table arrangements provide detail at guest level. The result is a considered setting that feels welcoming from the first course through to the evening celebration.
