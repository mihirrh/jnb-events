## 3. Technical & SEO Implementation Notes

1. **Responsive Image Optimization**:
   - Astro’s internal image pipeline (via Sharp) automatically generates WebP, AVIF, and high-density fallback variants for all screens during `astro build`.
   - All source images are retained at full resolution under `src/assets/media/`.

2. **Schema.org Structured Data**:
   - Every gallery project and service page automatically feeds image URLs, captions, and names into `ImageGallery`, `LocalBusiness`, and `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD schemas generated by `src/lib/schema.ts`.

3. **Regional SEO Keyword Coverage**:
   - The alt texts and accompanying page copy provide localized search signals for:
     - *Leicester* & *Leicestershire*
     - *Midlands* (East Midlands & West Midlands)
     - *United Kingdom* / UK-wide Asian and Hindu wedding markets.
